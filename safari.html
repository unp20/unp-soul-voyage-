The Heart of the Wild

Experience Tanzania's Untamed Beauty

From the legendary Great Migration in the Serengeti to the breathtaking Ngorongoro Crater, and the accessible wilderness of Mikumi — UNP Soul Voyage curates safaris that connect you with the soul of Africa. Explore Tanzania's five greatest national parks below.

Serengeti National Park

Witness the Great Migration and the endless plains of the Serengeti. Home to the Big Five.

Ngorongoro Crater

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Africa's greatest wildlife spectacles.

Tarangire National Park

Home to large elephant herds and iconic baobab trees.

Mikumi National Park

The perfect short safari from Dar es Salaam. Great for day trips.

Ruaha National Park

Tanzania's best-kept secret — wild, remote, and untouched.

Meet the Wildlife

Tanzania is home to some of the most iconic animals on Earth. Here are the magnificent creatures you may encounter on your safari.

Simba (Lion)

The King of Beasts — found in Serengeti, Ngorongoro, and Ruaha.

Tembo (Elephant)

Large herds roam Tarangire and Mikumi National Parks.

Twiga (Giraffe)

Elegant and tall — a common sight across all parks.

Pundamilia (Zebra)

Thousands migrate with the wildebeest in the Serengeti.

Nyati (Buffalo)

Part of the Big Five — powerful and found in large herds.

Chui (Leopard)

Elusive and beautiful — often seen in Serengeti and Ruaha.

The Maasai People

The Maasai are one of the most iconic indigenous groups in East Africa. Known for their distinctive red shukas, beadwork, and warrior traditions, the Maasai have lived alongside wildlife in Tanzania and Kenya for centuries.

Visiting a Maasai village is a cultural experience like no other. Learn about their traditions, enjoy their dances, and see how they coexist with the wild. UNP Soul Voyage can arrange authentic Maasai cultural visits as part of your safari.
