Nyasix Irie Sas: The Inner Rasta Spirit

Nyasix Irie Sas is the spirit within Rasta. It is the spiritual force that guides our daily lives. Rasta Fe Brook Irie Sas represents this inner identity, bringing peace, love, and self-awareness. This is not just a religion; it is a lifestyle that liberates us economically and spiritually.

UNP Artisan Treasure: Rastafari Fashion & Motivation

UNP Artisan Treasure is a collection of Rastafari Fashion Business and Motivation. We sell Rasta fashion items, handmade crafts, and music that reflects African culture. Beyond business, we provide motivation to the youth so they can liberate themselves economically through their talents and promote Tanzanian tourism.

Tourism & Artistic Life

We promote Tanzanian tourism by connecting visitors with our natural culture. Through art and music, we provide a platform for artists, photographers, and creatives to develop youth talents and showcase Rastafari culture to the world.
