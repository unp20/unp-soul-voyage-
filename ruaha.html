RUAHA NATIONAL PARK

Tanzania's Best-Kept Secret

Discover Ruaha

Ruaha National Park is the largest national park in Tanzania and one of the wildest, most remote safari destinations in Africa. It is famous for its large population of elephants, lions, and wild dogs — and for being far less crowded than the northern parks. If you want an authentic, untouched African wilderness experience, Ruaha is the place.

Wild & Remote

One of the least visited parks in Tanzania — a true wilderness experience.

10% of Lions

Home to approximately 10% of the world's remaining lion population.

Great Ruaha River

The lifeblood of the park, attracting wildlife from miles around.

Activities in Ruaha

Game Drives

Explore the vast plains, riverbeds, and miombo woodlands with expert guides.

Walking Safaris

Experience the wild on foot — the ultimate safari adventure.

Bird Watching

Over 570 bird species — including migratory birds from Europe and Asia.

Night Game Drives

See nocturnal animals like leopards, genets, and bushbabies.
